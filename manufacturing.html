How We Manufacture

At Sky Gold Steel, every product is manufactured using premium-grade steel, advanced machinery, and strict quality standards. Our experienced team ensures that every almirah and office furniture item is durable, strong, and professionally finished.

Manufacturing Steps

1. Steel Selection

Only high-quality steel sheets are selected for manufacturing.

2. Cutting & Bending

Precision cutting and bending using modern machines.

3. Welding

Strong welding for maximum durability and long life.

4. Powder Coating

Premium powder coating for a smooth, rust-resistant finish.

Quality Control

Every product passes through multiple quality inspections before dispatch. We check material strength, welding quality, surface finish, locking system and overall durability to ensure every customer receives a premium product.

Packaging & Delivery

Products are loaded with proper handling for secure delivery.

Fast Delivery
